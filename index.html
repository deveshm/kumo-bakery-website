♡   Care Instructions   ♡

How to Handle Your Cake

Because our chiffon cakes use 100% natural fresh cream with no stabilisers, please follow these five simple rules.

☁️
Kumo Craft Bakery
♡
CAKE CARE
instructions
🚗
Keep Flat

Car floor only (not the seat). Place the box on a completely flat, level surface.

🏎️
Drive Carefully

Go slow. Avoid sudden brakes & sharp turns throughout the journey.

❄️
Keep Cool

A/C on full cool. Position the cake in the footwell area with direct cold airflow.

🏠
After Pickup

Go straight home and refrigerate immediately (2°C – 5°C). Enjoy within 2–3 days.

🛡️
Transportation Notice

Once the cake leaves my hands, I'm unable to take responsibility for damages during transportation. Thank you for understanding!
